## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an executive-grade operational environment for multi-tenant B2B enterprise software and workspace management. Designed for founders, financial controllers, and operations directors handling complex fiscal, inventory, and cross-company logistics, the platform balances high-density data utility with modern SaaS minimalism.

The aesthetic fuses contemporary digital ergonomics with the refined nuance of traditional Japanese color discipline (*dentō-shoku*). The UI avoids decorative, thematic, or historical clichés: Japanese influence is expressed strictly through calibrated chroma, measured optical weight, and subtle surface balance. 

The resulting experience evokes institutional composure, clarity under operational velocity, and minimal cognitive strain during sustained sessions. Copy, metrics, and localization adhere to Argentine Spanish (`es-AR`), maintaining direct, clear operational instructions without informal colloquialisms.

## Colors

The color palette translates the measured restraint of traditional Japanese pigments (*dentō-shoku*) into a functional SaaS hierarchy. 

### Core Hierarchy & Foundations

#### Light Mode
- **App Background (Gofun-iro)**: `#FFFFFC`. A warm mineral shell that softens eye strain compared to harsh optical whites.
- **Surface / Cards**: `#FFFFFF` bounded by an exact 1px subtle stroke (`#EAEAEA`).
- **Primary (Ruri-iro)**: `#1E50A2`. Lapis lazuli deep blue used for primary CTA buttons, active tab indicators, links, and selected navigational states.
- **Accent (Momo-iro)**: `#F09199`. Peach blossom pink applied intentionally for focused highlights, badges, and secondary analytical charts.
- **Accent Soft Container (Sakura-iro)**: `#FEF4F4`. Pale petal tint used for active chip states, notification badges, and highlighted table rows.
- **Text & Headings (Sumi)**: `#1F2033`. Deep indigo-tinted ink black providing maximum readability without monochromatic harshness.
- **Secondary & Meta Text**: `#5E6478`. Balanced slate neutral for metadata, sub-labels, and field descriptors.

#### Dark Mode
- **App Background (Kon-iro)**: `#0F1A33`. Deep oceanic navy creating an expansive base.
- **Surface / Cards**: `#1A2747`. Elevated container tone providing distinct separation against the canvas.
- **Primary (Wasurenagusa-iro)**: `#89C3EB`. Luminous forget-me-not blue engineered for accessible contrast on dark containers.
- **Accent (Toki-iro)**: `#F4B3C2`. Soft crested ibis pink for critical indicators, tag highlights, and secondary metrics.
- **Text & Headings**: `#F3F1F6`. Soft off-white preventing halation and visual fatigue.
- **Secondary Text**: `#9AA5BE`. Subdued periwinkle slate for secondary lines and table headings.
- **Border Subtle**: `#23335A`. Crisp 1px definition separating surfaces.

### Accessibility Constraints
- **Pink/Accent Usage**: Never use `#F09199` or `#F4B3C2` for body copy, table cell text, or regular labels on light backgrounds. Reserve them strictly for badges, graph series, and decorative accents over soft container fills.
- **Semantic Feedback**:
  - **Success (Tokiwa-iro)**: `#2E7D5B` (Evergreen muted teal-green) for positive margins, paid invoices, and active company statuses.
  - **Warning (Yamabuki)**: `#C88A35` (Muted warm amber-gold) for pending authorizations and expiring certificates.
  - **Error (Beni-hi)**: `#C93A40` (Subdued crimson red) for fiscal discrepancies, rejected batches, and destructive alerts.
  - **Info**: `#3B72AF` in light mode; `#89C3EB` in dark mode.

## Typography

The typography relies on `Inter` to provide structural rhythm and readability across dense interfaces.

### Numerical and Financial Standards
- All currency balances, CUIT / CUIL identifiers, invoice numbers, and analytical data must use tabular numerals (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`).
- Numerical values follow Argentine standards: period as thousands separator and comma as decimal separator (`$ 1.450.200,80`). Negative values are formatted with an explicit minus sign (`- $ 32.400,00`).

### Roles and Application
- **KPI Metrics**: Display high-value monetary balances with strict line-height parity to prevent baseline jumps when paired with inline trend badges.
- **Data Table Cells**: Standardized on `body-md` (13px/18px) to allow high vertical information density without compromising cross-row scanning.
- **Labels & Column Headers**: Rendered in `label-sm` with 0.03em letter-spacing in uppercase or title-case to clearly demarcate metadata containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a fluid frame designed for high-density administrative operations, consisting of a fixed shell and an adaptive canvas.

### Shell Dimensions
- **Left Collapsible Sidebar**: 260px expanded, collapsing to 68px. Transitions take 200ms with smooth standard easing.
- **Top Navigation Bar**: Fixed at 56px height, positioned with sticky elevation above the work canvas.
- **Work Canvas**: Dynamically scales across `calc(100vw - sidebar_width)` with a maximum content ceiling of 1600px centered on ultrawide viewports.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column layout, 24px margins, 16px to 24px gutters. KPI metric decks display 4 columns per row.
- **Compact Desktop / Tablet Landscape (1024px – 1279px)**: 12-column layout, 16px gutters. Metric decks adapt to 2 or 4 columns. Sidebar defaults to collapsed state (68px).
- **Tablet Portrait (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout. Sidebar transitions to an off-canvas drawer overlay (`z-index: 50`). Data tables enforce horizontal scrolling with pinned leading columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, 16px margins. Metric cards stack vertically. Contextual actions move into bottom sheets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through subtle tonal layering and crisp 1px borders, avoiding heavy drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Application Canvas)**: Flat baseline on `#FFFFFC` (Light) or `#0F1A33` (Dark).
- **Level 1 (Card Containers, Tables, In-Canvas Panels)**:
  - *Light Mode*: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#EAEAEA`, subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(31, 32, 51, 0.04)`.
  - *Dark Mode*: Background `#1A2747`, 1px solid `#23335A`, ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Select Menus, Flyouts)**:
  - *Light Mode*: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#DFDFDF`, elevation: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 32, 51, 0.08)`.
  - *Dark Mode*: Background `#1A2747`, 1px solid `#2E416E`, elevation: `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Command Center `⌘K`)**:
  - *Light Mode*: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#D0D0D0`, elevation: `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(31, 32, 51, 0.12)`.
  - *Dark Mode*: Background `#1A2747`, 1px solid `#374E85`, elevation: `0 20px 40px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances crisp geometric precision with approachable software polish:

- **Form Controls & Action Buttons (8px / `0.5rem`)**: Standard for text inputs, select triggers, interactive buttons, and table rows.
- **Cards, Panels & Overlays (12px / `0.75rem`)**: Applied to metric KPI tiles, data table shells, flyout panels, and modal containers.
- **Pill Badges (`9999px`)**: Reserved exclusively for status indicators, quantitative count pills, and segmented tab toggles.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Ruri-iro (`#1E50A2` light / `#89C3EB` dark). Text `#FFFFFF` (light) or `#0F1A33` (dark). Hover adjusts 8% darker in light, 8% lighter in dark. Height: 36px (dense) or 40px (default). Radius: 8px. Focus ring: 2px with 2px offset.
- **Secondary**: Light mode uses `#FFFFFF` background with `#EAEAEA` border and `#1F2033` text. Dark mode uses `#1A2747` background with `#23335A` border and `#F3F1F6` text. Hover adds subtle background tinting.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background, `#5E6478` text. Hover transitions to `#FEF4F4` with `#1E50A2` text in light mode, or `#23335A` in dark mode.
- **Destructive**: Subdued crimson `#C93A40` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for irreversible actions (*"Eliminar comprobante"*, *"Desvincular sucursal"*).

### 2. Form Inputs & Selects
- Height: 36px (compact operational) or 40px.
- Light mode: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border `#EAEAEA`, text `#1F2033`. Placeholder in `#5E6478`.
- Dark mode: `#141F3B` fill, 1px border `#23335A`, text `#F3F1F6`. Placeholder in `#9AA5BE`.
- Active focus state: Border transitions to `#1E50A2` (light) or `#89C3EB` (dark) with a soft 3px focus glow ring.

### 3. Status Chips & Badges
Pill shapes (`rounded-full`), height: 22px, font size: 11px, weight: 600, padding: 2px 8px, containing a 6px status dot:
- **Activo / Liquidado**: Light: `#EDF6F1` background, `#2E7D5B` text and dot. Dark: `#122E23` background, `#4ADE80` text and dot.
- **Pendiente / Revisión**: Light: `#FBF5EB` background, `#C88A35` text and dot. Dark: `#38260D` background, `#FBBF24` text and dot.
- **Inactivo / Rechazado**: Light: `#FDF2F2` background, `#C93A40` text and dot. Dark: `#3B1316` background, `#F87171` text and dot.
- **Borrador / Informativo**: Light: `#EFF5FC` background, `#3B72AF` text and dot. Dark: `#132640` background, `#89C3EB` text and dot.
- **Destacado (Accent Momo)**: Light: `#FEF4F4` background, `#C85A65` text (ensuring 4.5:1 contrast against light background; never use raw `#F09199` for small text), `#F09199` dot.

### 4. Cards & Metric Displays
- Minimum height: 120px. Padding: 16px. Border radius: 12px.
- Structure: Top row features the section label (`label-sm`, secondary text) and action icon. Center holds the primary figure (`kpi-metric`). Bottom shows delta trends with inline directional arrows.

### 5. Data Tables
- **Header**: Height 36px. Light: `#F7F7F4` background, 1px bottom border `#EAEAEA`. Dark: `#141F3B` background, 1px bottom border `#23335A`. Text formatted with `label-sm`.
- **Body Rows**: Height 44px (dense). Alternating hover highlight (`#FEF4F4` at 40% opacity in light mode, `#1E2D52` in dark mode).
- **Checkboxes**: 16x16px rounded-sm with `#1E50A2` fill on active state.

### 6. Tabs & Segmented Navigation
- **Underline Style**: 2px active indicator in `#1E50A2` (light) or `#89C3EB` (dark), aligned flush to container borders.
- **Segmented Pill Style**: Embedded within an inset tray (`#F4F4F0` light / `#131F3B` dark), active item highlighted in white container with an ambient drop shadow.

### 7. Multi-Workspace Shell
- **Top Bar (56px)**:
  - *Workspace Switcher*: Dropdown containing tenant business avatar, legal business name (Razon Social), and CUIT format (`CUIT 30-71123456-9`).
  - *Global Search*: Command trigger (`"Buscar comprobantes, productos, clientes... ⌘K"`).
  - *Utility Suite*: Theme toggle, notifications with Momo pink unread indicator, and user account menu.
- **Sidebar (260px / 68px)**:
  - Header: Logo icon with brand label and status badge (`"Producción"`).
  - Navigation Items: 20px monochrome icon paired with title:
    1. *Panel Principal*
    2. *Empresas & CUITs*
    3. *Facturación & AFIP*
    4. *Listas de Precios*
    5. *Inventario & Stock*
    6. *Clientes & Proveedores*
    7. *Reportes & Balances*
    8. *Configuración*
  - Active item indicator: Left 3px accent bar with matching background wash (`#FEF4F4` light, `#1E2F56` dark).
  - Footer: Collapse trigger button (`"Contraer menú"` / `"Expandir menú"`).
